4. Doppler Effect & Walking

When a person walks, the reflected waves get compressed or expanded. Different body parts move at different speeds, creating a micro-Doppler signature (a unique physical fingerprint of motion). The Doppler frequency shift is intrinsically derived from the time-derivative of the phase.

Mathematical Derivation

Doppler from Phase Derivative

Let the target distance be a function of time: $d(t) = d_0 + vt$. The instantaneous round-trip phase of the reflected wave is:

$$ \phi(t) = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda} (2d(t)) = \frac{4\pi}{\lambda} (d_0 + vt) $$

Instantaneous angular frequency $\omega_D$ is defined as the time-derivative of the phase:

$$ \omega_D = \frac{d\phi}{dt} = \frac{4\pi v}{\lambda} $$

Converting angular frequency back to standard frequency ($f_D = \omega_D / 2\pi$), we obtain the exact Doppler shift:

$$ f_D = \frac{2v}{\lambda} $$

33. The EM Wave Equation

By decoupling Maxwell's equations in empty space, we prove that electromagnetic waves are self-propagating and physically mandate the speed of light.

Mathematical Derivation

Deriving the Speed of Light

In an empty vacuum, there is no charge ($\rho = 0$) and no current ($\vec{J} = 0$). We take the vector curl ($\nabla \times$) of Faraday's Law:

$$ \nabla \times (\nabla \times \vec{E}) = \nabla \times \left( -\frac{\partial \vec{B}}{\partial t} \right) = -\frac{\partial}{\partial t} (\nabla \times \vec{B}) $$

We substitute the Ampere-Maxwell law for $\nabla \times \vec{B}$ into the right side:

$$ \dots = -\frac{\partial}{\partial t} \left( \mu_0 \epsilon_0 \frac{\partial \vec{E}}{\partial t} \right) = -\mu_0 \epsilon_0 \frac{\partial^2 \vec{E}}{\partial t^2} $$

For the left side, we apply the foundational Vector Calculus identity: $\nabla \times (\nabla \times \vec{E}) = \nabla(\nabla \cdot \vec{E}) - \nabla^2 \vec{E}$. Since Gauss's Law in a vacuum states $\nabla \cdot \vec{E} = 0$, the first term vanishes, leaving $-\nabla^2 \vec{E}$.

Equating both sides and canceling the negatives yields the 3D Wave Equation:

$$ \nabla^2 \vec{E} = \mu_0 \epsilon_0 \frac{\partial^2 \vec{E}}{\partial t^2} $$

This takes the exact form of the classical wave equation $\nabla^2 u = \frac{1}{v^2} \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2}$, explicitly proving that light and WiFi propagate at velocity $v = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0 \epsilon_0}} = c$.

37. Singular Value Decomposition

SVD mathematically transforms messy, entangled multipath interference into clean, independent parallel communication streams by decomposing the complex Matrix shear into orthogonal geometric rotations.

Mathematical Derivation

SVD Channel Decoupling

The received signal vector $\mathbf{y}$ is heavily tangled by the multipath channel matrix $\mathbf{H}$:

$$ \mathbf{y} = \mathbf{H}\mathbf{x} + \mathbf{n} $$

By computing the Singular Value Decomposition of the channel, we factor it into $\mathbf{H} = \mathbf{U} \mathbf{\Sigma} \mathbf{V}^H$. We then intentionally "precode" our transmission by multiplying our data by $\mathbf{V}$ before sending it: $\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{V} \mathbf{\tilde{x}}$.

$$ \mathbf{y} = (\mathbf{U} \mathbf{\Sigma} \mathbf{V}^H) (\mathbf{V} \mathbf{\tilde{x}}) + \mathbf{n} $$

Because $\mathbf{V}$ is a unitary matrix, $\mathbf{V}^H \mathbf{V} = \mathbf{I}$ (it perfectly cancels out). The receiver then "postcodes" the arriving signal by multiplying it by $\mathbf{U}^H$:

$$ \mathbf{\tilde{y}} = \mathbf{U}^H \mathbf{y} = \mathbf{U}^H \mathbf{U} \mathbf{\Sigma} \mathbf{\tilde{x}} + \mathbf{U}^H \mathbf{n} \implies \mathbf{\tilde{y}} = \mathbf{\Sigma} \mathbf{\tilde{x}} + \mathbf{\tilde{n}} $$

Conclusion: Because $\mathbf{\Sigma}$ is strictly a diagonal matrix, all cross-talk interference is mathematically eradicated. The messy room becomes multiple, perfect, parallel pipes.

51. Information Geometry (The Ultimate Limit)

We end at the bleeding edge of theoretical statistics. Fisher Information measures the "sharpness" or geometric curvature of the probability distribution. High noise structurally flattens this curve, permanently raising the Cramér-Rao Lower Bound—the absolute physical limit on AI accuracy. No algorithm can beat this physical floor.

Mathematical Derivation

The Cramér-Rao Lower Bound

We want to estimate a physical parameter $\theta$ (like distance) from a noisy signal $X$. The Likelihood function is $f(X; \theta)$. The "Score" is the gradient of the log-likelihood: $\frac{\partial}{\partial \theta} \ln f(X; \theta)$.

The Fisher Information $\mathcal{I}(\theta)$ is defined as the variance of this score, which identically equals the negative expected value of the second derivative (the curvature):

$$ \mathcal{I}(\theta) = -\mathbb{E} \left[ \frac{\partial^2}{\partial \theta^2} \ln f(X; \theta) \right] $$

The Cramér-Rao theorem states that the variance of any unbiased estimator $\hat{\theta}$ (no matter how advanced the AI) is bounded by the inverse of the Fisher Information:

$$ \text{Var}(\hat{\theta}) \ge \frac{1}{\mathcal{I}(\theta)} $$

If the noise is high, the probability curve becomes flat. The second derivative (curvature) approaches zero. Therefore, $\mathcal{I}(\theta) \to 0$, causing the minimum possible error bound to approach infinity.
